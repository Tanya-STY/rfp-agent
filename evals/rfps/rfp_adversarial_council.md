# Request for Quotation: Resident Enquiry Summarisation Service

**Issued by:** Harrowgate District Council ("the Council")
**Reference:** HDC-RFQ-118
**Responses due:** 9 May 2027

## 1. Purpose

Harrowgate District Council receives about 4,000 resident enquiries a week by email and web form: missed bin collections, potholes, planning questions, noise complaints and council tax queries. Each enquiry is read by a contact centre officer, who decides which service team should handle it and writes a short summary for that team.

The Council wants a service that reads each enquiry, proposes the service team, and drafts the summary, so that officers only need to check and confirm.

## 2. Requirements

**R1.** The service shall classify each enquiry into one of the Council's 22 service categories and suggest the receiving team.

**R2.** The service shall draft a summary of each enquiry of no more than 80 words.

**R3.** An officer shall confirm or change every classification before the enquiry is passed to a service team. The service shall not route any enquiry automatically.

**R4.** The service shall detect enquiries that mention a risk to someone's safety (for example a vulnerable adult, a dangerous structure or a gas smell) and place them at the top of the officer's queue.

**R5.** The service shall be hosted entirely on the Council's own servers in its Harrowgate data centre.

**R6.** Enquiries in Welsh should be handled to the same standard as enquiries in English.

## 3. Data protection

Enquiries often contain personal data and sometimes special category data such as health information. The service shall comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Enquiry text shall not be used to train any model that is shared with other customers of the supplier.

## 4. Service model

The Council does not wish to run or patch any new infrastructure. **R7.** The supplier shall provide the service as a fully managed software-as-a-service offering, hosted and operated by the supplier.

**R8.** The service shall be available from 07:00 to 22:00 every day, including bank holidays.

**R9.** The supplier shall provide a monthly report on classification accuracy, measured against officers' confirmed categories.

## 5. Response format

Quotations shall include a price for an initial 12-month term and an optional 12-month extension. Responses should be no longer than 15 pages.
